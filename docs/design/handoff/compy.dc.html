decisions worth flagging

Words. shipped becomes built in to compy, shown as an icon. remote becomes the URL host. local gets nothing. variable set becomes preset. modified lives only where it acts: the sync icon and its tooltip.

Menu. one list, not two: configs with a preset submenu where one exists. picking a preset is the same gesture as activating, so nothing can be half-chosen. this menu is live — it drives the window beside it.

Against the brief. the per-config pinned binary is gone. one collector, chosen once in Settings, runs everything: no config or preset can disagree with another, and "which binary was this running under" stops being a question. adding alternatives stays possible.

Things to click. activate ebpf-profiles for the failure. the pencil opens a preset under its row. click a name for the yaml. save twice in the editor: the collector's complaint, then the quiet success. stop the collector on the Collector screen to reach the nothing-active state.

Still open. what "healthy" means beyond "the process started" — needed before anything can promise a known-good setup. and whether editing a built-in should fork a copy instead of taking it over.
